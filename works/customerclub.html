The Challenge

A loyalty programme nobody wanted to engage with

A regional bank's loyalty campaign had low redemption, high call centre volume, and users who genuinely didn't understand how the point system worked.

The existing system was invisible. Users accumulated points but had no way to track them, understand their value, or feel motivated to redeem. It felt like a chore rather than a reward. The business needed engagement, not just enrollment.

When the system is opaque, trust collapses. Users don't redeem because they don't believe their points are worth anything.
  • Point logic was hidden. Users couldn't see how many they had or how they were earned
  • No progress toward rewards. No visible path from current state to next milestone
  • High call centre volume from customers asking about balances and redemption
  • Redemption felt risky. Users worried they'd waste points on the wrong thing
  • No emotional hook. The experience felt like admin, not reward
The Strategic Pivot

From invisible programme to visible game

The core reframe: treat loyalty like a game with clear rules, visible progress, and satisfying milestones. Not a background process users had to trust blindly.

Before
Hidden point system. Users enrolled and forgot. No visibility, no motivation, no action.
→
After
Gamified Customer Club with transparent points, visible tiers, milestone celebrations, and a VIP dashboard.
The Solution

Four mechanics that made loyalty feel like a reward

Transparent Point Tracker

A real-time dashboard showing exact point balances, how they were earned, and what each point is worth. Eliminated the mystery that was driving call centre volume.

Tier Progression System

Three visible tiers (Silver, Gold, VIP) with clear criteria and a progress bar showing exactly how far away the next tier was. Progress creates pull.

Personalised Reward Recommendations

Surfaced the most relevant redemption options based on past behaviour. Users stopped feeling like they might waste points on the wrong reward.

Milestone Celebrations

Moment-of-delight animations on tier upgrades and first redemptions. Transformed the emotional experience from administrative to genuinely exciting.

The Impact

Loyalty that people actually used

2×
Redemption rate doubled
First month post-launch saw a 2× spike in redemptions. Transparency and personalisation removed the friction that had been suppressing engagement.
↓
Call centre volume dropped
Fewer customers calling to ask about balances and how points work. The dashboard answered those questions before they became calls.
↑
Repeat engagement
Users returned to check their progress toward the next tier, a behaviour the old system had never generated.
→
Platform became a selling point
The bank began using the Customer Club dashboard as a differentiator in new customer acquisition, not just a retention tool.
Key insight

"Loyalty systems fail when they're invisible. The moment users could see exactly where they stood and what they were working toward, the programme stopped feeling like a chore and started feeling like a game worth playing."
