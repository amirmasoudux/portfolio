The Catalyst

A configurator losing customers at every step

The legacy enterprise configurator forced users to navigate an opaque, all-at-once matrix of technical choices. High cognitive load, vague pricing transparency, and ambiguous steps resulted in critical funnel drop-offs and skyrocketing support costs.

As a customer attempting to configure a telecom package, users were confronted with too many options simultaneously, unclear real-time pricing, and no sense of progress. They felt anxious about making an incorrect choice, so they abandoned the flow completely or dialled into support out of sheer frustration.

"Why is this hard for people, and how do we fix it?" That question drove every decision.
  • Revenue Leakage: Severe funnel friction caused qualified buyers to drop off right before the final checkout stage
  • Diluted Campaign ROI: Expensive paid traffic was funnelled into an interface incapable of maintaining user intent
  • Escalating OpEx: Confused users repeatedly relied on support agents to complete simple package configurations
  • No Progress Visibility: Users had no sense of how far they had come or what remained in the flow
  • Cognitive Overload: All options presented simultaneously, violating Miller's Law and Hick's Law

The Execution

Design decisions that transformed the funnel

Progressive Disclosure

Swapped the single-page choice overload for a modular, multi-step layout. Choices are grouped into contextual bites: Infrastructure, Hardware, Add-ons. Eliminated sensory paralysis and stabilised user momentum throughout the configuration pipeline.

One contextual decision at a time

Real-time Pricing Summary

Designed a persistent, responsive sticky summary card tracking the base price, itemised choices, and recurring fees dynamically. Established radical transparency, neutralising the sticker shock and anxiety that triggered immediate cart abandonment.

Transparent pricing at every step

Clear Error Recovery

Created descriptive inline error handling and structural dependency warnings. For example, selecting Router B requires Line Connection X. Replaced cryptic system errors with humanised copy, preventing users from reaching frustrating dead-ends.

Humanised errors, no dead-ends

Contextual Up-sells

Positioned high-value add-ons like extra speed or premium safety bundles as clear choice blocks with explicitly mapped benefits. Enhanced Average Order Value organically without annoying users with dark-pattern popups.

Value-driven, not dark-pattern
