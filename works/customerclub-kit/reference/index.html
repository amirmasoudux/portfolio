Driving Loyalty

A regional bank gave away big prizes, from a car to smartphones and cash. But most customers didn't know they were in the draw, and many winners thought the bank's call was a scam. I designed a Customer Club, on the web and in the app, where members see their points, redeem them and follow the prize draws.

The problem

Informed, but not involved

The bank told customers about their points only by SMS, and many people never read it. Points also sat in a corner of the internet bank, where most people overlooked them. So the campaign rewarded people who didn't know they were taking part.

Invisible points

SMS went unread, and the points page in the internet bank was easy to miss. Most customers had no idea where they stood.

The wrong winners

Customers with large balances collected points without doing anything. The bank wanted to reward loyal customers: people who use their account and grow their savings.

A win felt like a scam

Winners called at the draw didn't believe it. Many called the call centre to check, and others called just to ask for their points.

Discovery

Three sources, one number, and no clear place where customers could see how it added up.

Concept

From a points system to a club

The goal was to turn a passive points system into an active Customer Club. Three ideas guided every screen.

Transparency

Visual progress bars show how each source adds to the total.

Exclusivity

A club that feels like a VIP space, not a standard bank page.

Active engagement

Missions, a lucky wheel and a prize draw give people a reason to come back.

The solution

One club, five moments

Scroll through a member's journey. The club works on the web and in the mobile app.

  1. 01

    Earn

    The home screen shows every way to collect points: account balance, online banking, card payments, a short survey or adding your interests.

  2. 02

    Track

    A breakdown by source and a progress chart replace the black box. Customers can check their points themselves, without calling the bank.

  3. 03

    Play

    In the Champions League, members complete missions, spin a daily lucky wheel and climb a monthly leaderboard. Missions push people toward digital banking.

  4. 04

    Redeem

    Points become cinema tickets, discount codes, charity donations or gifts. Members also join the Grand Prize Draw for a car, smartphones and cash, and see their entries and the countdown.

  5. 05

    Win

    Draw results appear in the club first. Winners see their prize, when the bank will call and a code to check on the call, so the good news doesn't feel like a scam.

Recreated in English and white-labeled as "Alpha Bank". Names and numbers are examples. Step 05 (Win) is a new concept added for this case study.

On the web

The same club on desktop

Points already had a page inside the internet bank, but people overlooked it. The club got its own home on the web, with the same design as the app: points, draws, the lucky wheel, missions and the leaderboard in one view.

Impact

Visibility drives engagement

OPERATIONS

Fewer calls

Fewer calls about loyalty status, because members could check it themselves.

ENGAGEMENT

Double-digit rise

Point redemptions rose by double digits in the first quarter after launch.

DIGITAL

More digital banking

Members used online banking more to raise their usage points.

SATISFACTION

Members felt recognised

In focus groups, members said the VIP-style club made them feel recognised.

Key insightWhen people can see their points, they use them. Making the points visible turned a struggling campaign into a successful one.
